How it works

1

Log in

Link your Minecraft account with a quick in-game message. No password needed.

2

Open a case

Spend balance on a case. The server picks the item from the case's drop table.

3

Keep or sell

Keep what you win or sell it back to fund your next case.

Provably fair

Before each open, the server commits to a secret seed by showing you its hash. You add your own client seed. The result comes from HMAC-SHA256 over both, so neither side can change the outcome after the fact. The seed is revealed after the round so you can verify it yourself.
